# Introduction
Welcom to *The Yggdrasil Book*. Yggdrasil is a ecosystem for creating, maintaining, and serving perception fo the environment through 3D Scene Graphs for autonomous systems.

## Who Yggdrasil is For
Yggdrasil is a general purpose by design, meaning, anyone can use it to map their specific needs and environment into an Yggdrasil tree. Yggdrasil's simple design and reach integrations makes it easy to use it for prototyping and research papers while its high performance output makes it a perfect fit for practical usecases.

## Why Choose Yggdrasil
Yggdrasil has a rich set of features and integrations that makes it easy to adapt to any downstream or upstream use case within the robotic ecosystem:

1. **Built-in Support for complex positional Queries**: Yggdrasil has built-in support for a suite of positional queries, such as the **nearest neighbor** family of queries, **field of view** queries, and so many other queries that are commonly used in most downstream tasks such as path planning and navigation.

1. **Advance Support for semantic Retrieval**: Yggdrasil also has first-class support for semantic retrieval on nodes and edges.

1. **Automatic State Propagation**: Yggdrasil ensures that the scene graph remains in a **consistent and valid state** at all times. Any modification to a specific node automatically triggers necessary updates and notifications across dependent branches of the tree. This automated propagation eliminates manual state management and ensures that all downstream consumers receive accurate, synchronized data.

1. **Generic Building Blocks**: Unlike other scene graphs, Yggdrasil's generic building blocks makes it easy to map any scenario into an Yggdrasil tree and removes the need for implementing a new 3D scene graph logic from scratch.

5. **Community-Driven & Rapidly Evolving**: Yggdrasil is actively maintained and built to grow alongside the robotics ecosystem. Because development is ongoing, the roadmap is highly responsive to user needs. If a specific integration or feature is missing for your use case, we encourage you to request it—new capabilities are frequently rolled out, ensuring the framework stays ahead of industry demands.
